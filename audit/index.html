    The product is rarely the problem. What the customer sees is.

    The businesses I work with have something real to sell, whether it is already selling or about to open. What holds the price down is everything the customer meets before they buy: a logo that could belong to anyone, flyers and labels that do not match it, a page that reads like a template, a price with an apology attached. None of that is a product fault, and no amount of ad spend fixes it. Spending more only puts the same impression in front of more people.

    This is the check I run on a business before I take the work on. Go through it honestly. If your score comes back low, you now know which part to fix, and you know it before you spend money finding out the hard way.
