# L'ábẹ́ Igi: TikTok-first revision

Internal. Supersedes the 8 to 12 minute episode and the 5 January launch in `season-1-plan.md`. Everything else in that plan stands.

## 1. What changes

The unit is no longer the episode. It is the **post**. An "episode" is a week of posts built from one question.

| Post type | Length | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **The Word** (ayán) | 15 to 25 s | One Yorùbá word or rule, said correctly. Highest save and share potential. Cheapest to make: one elder, one shot or two. |
| **The Question** | 45 to 90 s | The cold open plus the guest's answer. Hook is the question. |
| **The Proverb** | 30 to 45 s | The silent elder's proverb and the guest's pushback. |
| **Tààrà** | 30 to 60 s | The hard follow-up and the answer. The comment-bait post. |

One question produces 4 to 6 posts. Six questions produce about 30 posts, and the four types let you test which one the audience wants before you commit more.

## 2. Format rules

- **9:16 master.** Confirm in the Flow test that 9:16 is available and holds the same quality and consistency. If it is not, generate 16:9 and reframe, and note the cost.
- **Hook in the first 2 seconds.** The question appears as on-screen text and in the elder's voice before anything else.
- **Two-shot is the wrong frame in vertical.** Alternate single close-ups: asker, guest, silent elder. Cut every 3 to 5 seconds. Flow's 8-second clips fit this directly (8 to 12 shots make one 60 to 90 second post).
- **Captions on every post, two lines:** Yorùbá with full tone marks on top, English below. Every Yorùbá caption is **[Paul to confirm]**.
- **The ritual line is the interaction.** End each Question post with *Dáhùn ìbéèrè ìjìnlẹ̀*. Pin the question as a comment. Use viewers' answers as the next episode's raw material, and Stitch the best ones.
- **Disclose that it is AI-generated.** Use TikTok's AI-content label every time. Viewers are tired of unlabelled AI, and the honest label plus a visibly human, recorded Yorùbá voice is the difference. Say it once on the profile: "Animated with AI. Voices are human."

## 3. Why the human voice is the moat

Plenty of AI video is on TikTok. Almost none has correctly toned Yorùbá spoken by a real person. The animation is a commodity. The voice, the language moment and the cultural judgement are not. Put the voice first.

## 4. Cadence (solo, realistic)

- **Three posts a week** is enough to learn from. Not daily.
- Suggested week: Mon The Word, Wed The Question, Fri Tààrà (Proverb in the gap).
- A weekly batch session: write on Sunday, generate and cut on Monday and Tuesday, schedule the rest.

## 6. What to measure (no invented targets)

Set targets only after the first 10 posts. Until then record, per post:

- Hold at 3 seconds and average watch time.
- Completion.
- **Saves and shares** (more important than likes for this content).
- Comments that are answers to the question, not just reactions.

Test, in the first 10 posts:
1. Which post type holds best (Word, Question, Proverb, Tààrà).
2. Whether the two-line caption helps or hurts.
3. Whether the elders' voices, the language moment, or the guest drives the shares.

At the mid-February review, decide: double down on the best type, drop the worst, and only then spend on real guests.

## 7. Account and distribution

- **A separate show account**, not the PENNIO account. Check the handle is free before committing. The profile links to `pennio.agency`.
- Cross-post the same vertical posts to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Add the WhatsApp status share on every post, because it is how it will actually spread.
- **Long-form** (the 8 to 12 minute episodes) waits until a type proves itself. A compilation on YouTube is cheap once the clips exist.
- **Do not count on platform payouts.** Creator payout programmes are limited by country. Check current eligibility for your country before building any plan around them. As of my knowledge, Nigeria was not covered, so verify. The money here is sponsors and the school pack, not views.

## 8. School pack still works

A vertical clip works in a classroom and in a WhatsApp group. The six discussion guides and six cards still make the pack. Sell it once a few posts have proven the audience.

## 9. New risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Algorithm indifference to a niche | Four post types, test early, no sunk cost |
| "AI slop" backlash | Label it, lead with the human voice, never fake a guest |
| Comment-section pile-ons about Yorùbá correctness | Pin a correction policy, fix errors publicly, keep a second reviewer |
| Sacred imagery misread | Same rule: white cloth and manner, no real office or rite |
| Burnout from a posting treadmill | Three a week, batch weekly, bank five posts before launch |
| Consent for real guests in short, shareable clips | Release form names clips and reposts, guest approves before release |
