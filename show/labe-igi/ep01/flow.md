## STYLE (paste at the end of every prompt)

> Stylised 3D animated feature look, painterly, warm earth tones, white and deep indigo, one small orange accent. Soft natural light, gentle depth of field. Vertical 9:16, locked camera, 8 seconds. Audio: quiet ambient sound only, no speech, no music. No text, no captions, no logos, no signage, no real person's likeness, no neon or futuristic chrome.

## Lip-sync note

Flow cannot sync a mouth to a recorded Yorùbá voice. Where someone speaks, frame them **in profile, over the shoulder, from behind, in a wide, or with hands and reactions in focus**. Avoid held close-ups of a speaking mouth. The shots below follow this.

## Beat 1: the greeting (the 15-second ritual)

**S01. Entrance** · refs R3, R4 · VO: none (ambient)
> Wide locked shot of a backyard under a colossal ivory-ochre mushroom tree with a broad cap whose underside glows soft amber. A ten-year-old boy in a white t-shirt, indigo shorts, white trainers and a small backpack, earbuds round his neck, enters from the left along a red earth path, looks up at the cap in wonder, then walks toward two seated elders in white. Warm afternoon light.

**S02. The prostrate greeting** · refs R1, R2, R3, R4 · VO: the boy, Paul's words: *Baba Olú, Mama Olú... ẹ kún. ẹ ǹlẹ́, mo kíi yín...*
> Medium side-view shot. A ten-year-old boy in a white t-shirt and indigo shorts lowers himself slowly and respectfully to the swept red earth in a full prostrate greeting before two seated elders in white, arms stretched forward. Calm, reverent, gentle light. No close-up of his face.
> *Fallback if it renders badly:* a silhouette from behind, or a wide shot.

**S03. The elders' welcome** · refs R1, R2 · VO: the elders: *ọmọ 're, kaabo s'ábẹ igi olú*
> Two-shot in three-quarter view of a warm elderly man in a white agbada and fila cap and an elderly woman in a white buba and gele, both smiling, one lifting a hand to invite someone to rise. Amber light from above. Seen slightly from behind and to the side.

**S04. The blessing** · refs R1, R2, R3 · VO: the elders: *oó d'àgbà...*
> Medium shot from behind the boy as he rises, the two elders smiling and nodding warmly in front of him, amber light from the cap above. *(Add a hand on the shoulder only if Paul confirms the gesture. Otherwise leave it out.)*

## Beat 2: the child's question

**S05. He sits** · refs R3, R4, R5 · VO: none
> Medium shot. A ten-year-old boy sits on a low stool at a round wooden table under the glowing underside of a giant mushroom cap, taking a small tablet from his backpack and propping it on the table, the two elders leaning in with interest. Warm light.

**S06. The question** · refs R3, R4 · VO: the boy (English placeholder: *"Why is our place called Ibarapa? What does it even mean?"*; Paul renders in mixed Yorùbá)
> Over-the-shoulder shot from behind the elders toward the boy, who gestures with both hands as he asks, the tablet glowing on the table in front of him. Shallow focus on his hands.

## Beat 3: the first answer (the melon)

**S07. Mama Olú answers** · refs R2, R4 · VO: Mama Olú (*"The old ones say the name comes from a melon. Egusi Ibara."*; Paul renders)
> Profile shot of an elderly woman in white buba and gele, calm and sure, hands folded, looking toward a child just out of frame. Amber light on her face, the glowing cap above. Seen from the side.

**S08. The melon seeds** · ref R6 · VO: none
> Overhead close shot of a bowl of pale melon seeds on dark wood, a few seeds scattered beside it, a hand sliding a few into the light. Warm soft light. *(Use R6, a real photograph, as the reference.)*

## Beat 4: the seven

**S09. The slate** · refs R4, R5 · VO: none (names added as captions in the edit)
> Close shot of a transparent floating slate above a wooden table under a glowing mushroom cap; seven small points of soft warm light rise in a loose cluster on the slate. No writing, no letters.

**S10. Baba Olú** · refs R1, R4 · VO: Baba Olú (*"Seven towns carry the name together."*; Paul renders and supplies the spellings)
> Three-quarter view from behind and to the side of an elderly man in a white agbada and fila cap, one hand raised toward the floating slate, the other resting on his knee. Amber light.

## Beat 5: "That I do not know"

**S11. The pushback** · refs R3, R4 · VO: the boy (*"A melon? Why would a whole place be named after a melon?"*; Paul renders)
> Medium shot, over the shoulder, the boy sitting back with his hands turned out in comic disbelief, the tablet glowing beside him, the elders blurred in the background.

**S12. The pause** · refs R1, R4 · VO: Baba Olú (*"That I do not know. I will not make it up."*; Paul renders)
> Profile close shot of an elderly man in a white fila cap, quiet and honest, shaking his head slowly, one hand open on the table. Amber light from above. Seen from the side.

**S13. We will ask** · refs R2, R4 · VO: Mama Olú (*"The one who knows is the one who was there. We will ask."*; Paul renders)
> Wide shot. The elderly woman in white turns her head toward the horizon past the stalk, the dusk-gold threads of light stretching away across the red earth behind her. Calm.

## Beat 6: the proverb

**S14. The proverb** · refs R2, R3, R4 · VO: Mama Olú (the proverb: **Paul supplies it. I have not written one.**)
> Medium shot from behind the boy toward the elderly woman, who speaks with her hand raised gently, the boy listening with his chin in his hand. Warm light.

## Beat 7: close

**S15. The blessing again** · refs R1, R2, R3 · VO: the elders (*oó d'àgbà...*)
> Medium shot, the two elders smiling and lifting their hands in a quiet blessing toward the boy, who stands before them with his backpack on. Amber light.

**S16. He leaves** · refs R3, R4 · VO: none
> Wide shot from behind: the boy walks away along the red earth path, then stops and looks back up at the glowing underside of the giant mushroom cap. Warm light fading toward dusk.

**S17. End-card plate** · ref R4 · VO: none (captions and credit added in the edit)
