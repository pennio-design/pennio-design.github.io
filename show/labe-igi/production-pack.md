## 2. Premise

The tree is the school of the future. Two white-clad elders host. Each guest answers one deep question about how Africa learns next. The future grows out of the tradition: light in the branches, adire and aso-oke patterns moving in the air, a slate that floats. Nothing is neon or chrome.

Thesis for season 1: Africa's future of learning is built from the oral tradition, not around it.

## 6. Episode structure (8 to 12 minutes)

1. **Cold open.** The host says the ritual line, asks the one question.
2. **The Story.** The guest answers from experience.
3. **The Proverb.** The silent elder offers one. The guest agrees or fights it.
4. **The language moment.** One Yorùbá point in 20 seconds: an elision, an intensifier and the words it belongs with, a tonal pair.
5. **Tààrà.** The direct follow-up, the hard question.
6. **The close.** The guest gives one line to take away.

Each episode must contain at least one moment where the guest is funny, wrong or stubborn. Education comes second to entertainment.
