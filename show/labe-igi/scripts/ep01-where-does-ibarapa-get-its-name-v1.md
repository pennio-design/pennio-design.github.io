## Beats

**1. The greeting** (8 to 16 seconds). Visual: Flow prompts 8.3 and 8.4. Audio: the boy, in Paul's words:
*Baba Olú, Mama Olú... ẹ kún. ẹ ǹlẹ́, mo kíi yín...*
Elders: *ọmọ 're, kaabo s'ábẹ igi olú.* Then: *oó d'àgbà...*

**2. The child's question.** He sits at the table.
- Boy: "Why is our place called Ibarapa? What does it even mean?" **[mixed Yorùbá, Paul to render]**

**3. The first answer.** Mama Olú.
- "The old ones say the name comes from a melon. *Egusi Ibara.*" **[YÒRÙBÁ: Paul to render the elder's words]**
- On screen, an unlabelled shot of melon seeds in a bowl (Flow 6.1, real photo reference if possible).

**4. The seven.** Baba Olú.
- "Seven towns carry the name together." The slate floats up and lists them, spelled as Paul supplies. **[Paul to give the Yorùbá spellings and the exact name for the seven, *Ìbàràpá Méje* or the other form.]**

**5. The boy pushes back.**
- Boy: "A melon? Why would a whole place be named after a melon?" **[mixed Yorùbá]**
- Baba Olú, after a pause: "That I do not know. I will not make it up." **[YÒRÙBÁ: Paul to render]**
- Mama Olú: "The one who knows is the one who was there. We will ask." (Cut to the end card.)

**6. The proverb.** Mama Olú offers one about where a name comes from. **[YÒRÙBÁ: Paul to supply the proverb and its meaning. I will not invent one.]**

**7. The close.** The elders bless the boy (*oó d'àgbà...*, as typed above). The boy leaves, looking back up at the Igi Olú.

**End card (English captions, Paul's spelling for any Yorùbá):**
- "Told by [named bearer], [town], with permission." **[Paul to confirm his own name on the card, or choose someone else.]**
- A line to the audience: "If you know how Ibarapa got its name, tell the tree." (In English. The Yorùbá call to the viewer, if any, is Paul's: *Dáhùn ìbéèrè ìjìnlẹ̀* is held in reserve for this.)

## What this gives (cuts for TikTok)

1. **The greeting** (15 s). Pure ritual.
2. **"Why is Ibarapa called Ibarapa?"** (45 to 60 s). The question, the melon, the seven.
3. **"That I do not know."** (30 s). The honesty beat. A show that says out loud what it does not know is rare, and this is the hook that earns trust.
