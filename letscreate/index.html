Make real work. Get honest feedback. Grow in public.

Let's Create is a learning group I run for African creatives. You work on real projects, share rough drafts, and get honest feedback.

Start with a 7-day challenge. Join a 10-week cohort. Stay in the group after.

Apply for the 10-Week Cohort
How to join

Three steps to join.

You do not need perfect work to start. You move up one step at a time.

1. The 7-Day Challenge

One week of daily tasks. It shows me how you think and how serious you are.

2. Live Feedback Session

A live session where I go through your rough work with you and say what to fix.

3. 10-Week Cohort

Ten weeks working in a small team on a real project, with deadlines and feedback every week.

You have the talent. What you are missing is someone to show you how the work gets done.

A studio gives you a schedule, honest feedback, and proof of what you made. Most creatives learn alone and get none of that. This group gives you all three.

  • Talented people are learning alone.
  • Feedback is rare, or too polite to help.
  • Good work often goes unseen, so it never turns into real opportunity.
What you get

Three parts.

Everything here is built on one idea: people improve faster when they work with a team, a deadline and honest feedback.

01

Cohort Studio

Short cohorts where people with different skills work together like a real studio. You finish with a better way of working and a project you can show.

  • Design, writing, strategy and production.
  • Real projects, deadlines and weekly feedback.
  • Projects can be campaigns, brands, content, video or digital products.
02

Community Network

A group you stay in after the cohort ends, so you keep the people and the feedback.

  • Review each other's work and share what you learn.
  • You can join later cohorts.
  • A group to belong to, not a one-time class.
03

Opportunities

As the group grows, I want strong work to reach people who can hire it: reviews, visibility and selected work opportunities.

  • Reviews and design sprints.
  • People move up based on the quality of their work.
  • Opportunities go to people whose work has been seen and trusted.
How learning works

Build real work, not practice work.

Teams move from idea to design to writing to feedback to final polish, until the project is ready to show.

  • Weeks 1–2: Get started and form teams.
  • Weeks 3–5: Plan the project and come up with ideas.
  • Weeks 6–8: Make it, with feedback every week.
  • Weeks 9–10: Finish the work and show it publicly.
After the cohort

Keep growing in public.

After the cohort, you stay. Your work, your contacts and your progress stay with the group, and you can help the people coming in next.

  • People with different skills work together.
  • Your progress is on record.
  • Opportunities come because people can see your work.

Apply

Apply if you are ready to show your work, even unfinished.

This is for people who want to grow by doing real work and taking honest feedback. I read every application.

This is for you if
  • You want to learn by building inside a team.
  • You care about good thinking, not just finishing.
  • You can give real time to it for 10 weeks.
  • You want honest feedback so your work gets better.
Who should wait
  • You want a casual community with no deadlines.
  • You want praise and no changes.
  • You cannot work with a team on a schedule.
  • You are not sure yet that you want to do this seriously.
The first cohorts are small on purpose. I choose people carefully.

I read every application myself. I do not send spam or share your details.

I reply by email if there is a fit.
