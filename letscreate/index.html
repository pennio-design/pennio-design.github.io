Join the 7-day challenge
Seven days

Join the 7-day challenge.

One small task a day. Share your rough work, and each day I go through three on video.

  1. Day 1 Write what your work stands for, in one sentence.
  2. Day 2 Lay everything you have made side by side.
  3. Day 3 Pick two colours and two fonts. Say why.
  4. Day 4 Write the rules: what is allowed, what is not.
  5. Day 5 Say your price out loud, with no apology.
  6. Day 6 Look at your page for five seconds. What does it sell?
  7. Day 7 Remove your name from your last five posts. Could someone tell they are yours?

I will email you the date, then the first task on the morning it starts.

How to join

Three steps.

You do not need perfect work to start.

1. The 7-Day Challenge

One week of daily tasks. It shows me how you think.

2. Live Feedback Session

I go through your rough work with you and say what to fix.

3. 10-Week Cohort

Work in a small team on a real project, with feedback every week.

You have the talent. You are missing someone to show you how the work gets done.

Learning alone is hard, with little feedback and nothing to show for it. This group gives you a schedule, honest feedback and work you can show.

What you get

Three parts.

How learning works

Real work, not practice.

Teams go from idea to design to writing to feedback to final polish.

  • Weeks 1–2: Get started and form teams.
  • Weeks 3–5: Plan the project and come up with ideas.
  • Weeks 6–8: Make it, with feedback every week.
  • Weeks 9–10: Finish the work and show it publicly.
