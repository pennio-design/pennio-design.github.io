Why good businesses still look small.

What holds a good business back is usually what the customer sees before they buy.

01
It starts and ends with a logo
A logo gets designed before anyone decides what the business is about, and that is where it stops. Nobody decides how the flyers, labels or posts should look.
02
Everything after it is made by whoever is free
The flyer from one person, the labels from the printer, the shop banner from the signwriter, the posts from a phone at night. Each picks their own colour and their own font.
03
Ads cost more than they should
You spend more and get fewer customers. The ad looks nothing like your page, and the page looks like a template, so people do not trust where they land.
04
You start all over. Again.
Sales go down, so you change the logo. Everything you already printed and posted goes to waste.
Your business does not look small because it is small.
It looks small because nothing matches.
Your logo, your flyers, your labels, your shop front and your posts were made by different people at different times, and none of them were told what the others looked like. The customer feels the gap even when she cannot name it. She thinks the business is not sure of itself, so she expects to pay less.

Brilla
Cleaning service · Brand identity, creatives and website

A cleaning business getting ready to open, with nothing yet for a customer to look at.

What was going wrong

The founder had a real service and nothing to show for it. The first customer to ask would have had nothing to go on, because there was nothing a customer could look at and judge.

The question was not which post to design first. It was what the business needed to look like before any post could mean anything.

The question that shaped everything

Who pays for cleaning, and why do they choose one company over another?

Homes want it to feel safe. Offices want it to feel reliable. Those pull in different directions, and a brand that tries to be both ends up being neither.

The decision

I chose reliable as the feeling to design around. Offices buy on schedule and consistency, not on warmth, and that one choice set the mark, the colour, the look of the posts and the way the website is laid out and written.

What I did

The business came to me with its name already chosen. From there I made the brand identity (logo, colours, fonts and the rules for using them), directed the social media designs from start to finish, wrote the website copy, and built and shipped the site.

Questions before
booking the call.

What do you actually do?

I build brand identities: the name, the logo, the colours, the fonts, and the rules for using them. I build websites on top of that. I do not do one-off logos.

How much does it cost?

Brand identity starts at ₦350,000. With a website built on it, the pair starts at ₦650,000. A website on its own starts at ₦250,000 for a landing page, and online stores and larger sites are priced after the call. You get a written price before any work starts.

I am just starting. Is this too early?

No, if you have done your homework. Brilla launched with every asset ready before it opened, which is the easiest time to get a brand right. Before we start I will ask who buys, why they would pick you over the next option, and what you will charge. If you are still testing whether the idea works, do that first.

Do you work outside Nigeria?

Yes. Most of the work happens over calls and shared files.

I already have a logo and a website.

That is where most people start. On the call I look at what exists. If what you have is solid, I build on it instead of replacing it, and that costs you less.

What happens after the call?

We agree what the project is, I send a written price and timeline, and nothing starts until you have both.

How does payment work?

Half before work starts and the rest on delivery. Two rounds of revisions are included. If a project goes quiet for 10 days, I pause it. After 30 days I close it, and the deposit is not refunded. Quotes are valid for 14 days.
